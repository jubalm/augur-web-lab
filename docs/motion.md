# Website motion

Implementation timings live in `src/motion/timelines.ts`, derived from Figma `121:152`, `250:717`, the component state frames and the owner briefs. Since 6 October 2026 the looping scenes are choreographies shared with Figma: `src/motion/choreography.ts` defines tracks (keys on a 12 s cycle, in ms, opening and closing on the static artwork, each key's easing shaping the segment that arrives at it, as Figma does); `tenet-choreography.ts` and `hero-choreography.ts` generate them, and the same keys were written into Figma Section details `250:717` and `382:848`. Since the polish brief of 5 October 2026 the tenet diagrams loop while visible and the hero keeps a measured ambient loop; other scenes still play once. Owner feedback the same evening made the tenet loops continuous (always moving, no rest), made entrance scenes once per tab session so F5 never replays them, added a FAQ opening transition, rebuilt the header motion and marked superseded walkthrough stages. On 6 October 2026 Figma Section details was brought back in step: `250:717` (tenet loops, 24 s timeline), `382:848` (hero story loop, 12 s), the pointer-lens and tap-ripple states beside it, and the REP stake-flow tiles in section 07 carry the website's keys. Browser frame captures and any differences belong in `decisions.md`.

## Shared policy

`play-once.ts` starts a scene when it enters the viewport and cleans up after completion/pagehide. Interaction settles the region; reduced motion cancels it to the static end state. Content and art are fully visible before enhancement and remain settled with JavaScript disabled or in print. Only transform, opacity and SVG stroke draws animate. The picker may change layout only as an intentional interaction with an instant fallback. No scroll hijacking or information gated by animation.

Entrance scenes use the shared play-once controller and play once per tab session. A reload (F5) or a back/forward visit shows every entrance scene settled, read from the navigation type (`PerformanceNavigationTiming.type`), so it holds where `sessionStorage` is blocked: the 6 October report came from a browser that denies storage, where the earlier storage-only rule replayed everything on F5. On a fresh visit `seen.ts` records each scene (by page and script order) in `sessionStorage` when it starts, so a later visit in the same session shows it settled while scenes not yet reached still play; without storage a fresh visit plays them. The hero arrival records `augur:hero-arrived` and also skips on any non-`navigate` navigation type. Two exceptions play on every load, a reload included, at the owner's request (6 Oct 2026): the hero's “decentralized” underline sweep and the answer-band ribbon, which sweeps when it scrolls into view. Looping scenes use `loop-scene.ts`: a scene runs whenever any part of it is on screen, pauses off-screen or in a hidden tab and resumes where it was; every track starts and ends on the static artwork, runs linearly with easing on the keyframes, and may be offset by a positive delay (before its first iteration it shows the static artwork). Development-only `?motion=slow` multiplies timings by five; `?motion=replay` assists comparison. Production ignores those flags.

Click or tap in the hero (not on a control, not ending a text selection): a 1 px Green square ripple scales from 20% to 700% while fading (900 ms), and a Green front crosses the field at 0.75 px/ms; each square within 560 px flashes Green (760 ms) and pops to 130% (560 ms, composited on top of the loop). The background field still drifts ±6/±3 px on a 24 s CSS loop.

Pointer glow is limited to fine pointers and a 200 px radius, rAF-throttled; 120 ms in / 450 ms out. It resets on leave, scroll and resize, and is disabled for reduced motion, hidden document or off-screen hero. Only a subset of twelve heritage squares twinkles on varied 2.6–5.4 s opacity cycles. This is the sole ongoing ambient treatment and must be measured; off-screen/hidden pauses and reduced-motion cancellation apply.

## Who decides

The answer band is live text. When 60% of it is visible it sweeps in once per load (900 ms, cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1)), a reload or return visit included (owner, 6 Oct 2026: it was never seen while reviewing by refreshing): a Green ribbon grows from the left under the statement, led by three 10 px squares that fade over the last 180 ms; text ahead of the edge stays Paper on Navy and behind it Navy on Green. The diagrams are always moving while visible (owner, 5–6 Oct 2026: "more dynamic", "alive"). Every element has its own track on a 12 s cycle from `tenet-choreography.ts`; Figma `250:717` carries the same keys on a 24 s timeline (the start, with the Permissionless lead-ins, and one steady cycle), generated from the same tracks.

Squares pulse (owner pick, 6 Oct 2026, from four sketched treatments): every square that lights up pops to 116% (up over 140 with the reveal curve, back by 420 with crossfade) and sends out a 1 px outline of itself, an invisible square at rest, that grows to 175% (700, reveal) while it fades from 85% (40 up, 660 down) and then snaps back. Paper outlines for seated members brightening and Trustless senders; Green for crossers as they cross the edge, mesh squares where the relay lands and Trustless receivers. Trustless senders and receivers keep their 118% and 116% pops. The squares stay flat Mist at rest; no glow.
