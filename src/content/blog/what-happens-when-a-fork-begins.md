## The last resort

A fork only happens when a dispute escalates all the way. Challenges keep doubling the bond until it crosses the fork threshold.

## The universe splits

One child universe is created for each possible answer, each with its own REP. A fork creates outcome universes with one-way REP migration.

## Different designs

Augur v2’s completed fork, Augur Lituus’s proposed rules and Zoltar’s model differ in their details.
